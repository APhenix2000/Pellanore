The world at a glance

Where the story stands

Full campaign spoilers This encyclopedia contains events through Session 25. Session 26 currently contains only date placeholders.

Pellanore is a world of distinct nations held together by an ancient council, a divine castle, and the failing Sacred Vows. The heroes who met at a wedding in Didgeridoo now face the Dark Sentinels, the regime of the Light Weaver, and the possible return of The Darkness—known elsewhere as Cthulhu.

The active party

  • Grim — dwarf barbarian and prince of Ythguard
  • Diablo Dan — vigilante, now revealed as Malrackier Murtock
  • Iriel — druid with a divine history
  • Larkaid — ice-touched fey prince and keeper of Nymphina
  • Millicent — wererat cleric and champion of Talona
  • Wendyl — deep gnome ranger, also known as Stone
  • Xyphiro — dragonborn bard carrying Thelly's Soul
  • Persephone — rogue, resistance leader, and Queen of Hope

The central crisis

  1. The six Dark Sentinels are agents of The Darkness.
  2. The Sacred Vows once contained that cosmic threat; at least one vow is broken.
  3. The Light Weaver controls Aldara and is probably the Shadow Weaver's public identity.
  4. Ythguard, Borrigale, and Pullywicks form the strongest open resistance bloc.
  5. Aerodeth (Castle in the Sky) is partially repaired and may become the party's base.
  6. Grim has made direct contact with Cthulhu, creating a new and dangerous uncertainty.
